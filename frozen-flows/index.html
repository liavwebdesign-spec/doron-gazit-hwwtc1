Frozen Flows

Scroll

The Frozen Flows is a collection of sculptural lighting inspired by fluidity in nature and Doron’s decades long fascination with the invisible movement of wind and light in nature. His Frozen Flows capture and freeze the undulating rhythm found within them.
He developed a new technique of manipulating plastic resins which allows him to achieve very organic shapes. Each one is unique and no two designs are created the same.
